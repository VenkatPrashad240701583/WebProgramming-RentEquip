THE CAMPUS CHALLENGE
Problem Statement & Motivation
02 / 06
Why Do Students Need RentEquip?
Exam & Lab Emergencies:

Students frequently forget mandatory gear like lab coats, calculators, or drafters right before sessions, risking attendance or test entry.

High Cost for Single-Semester Use:

Purchasing a ₹1,500+ Casio fx-991EX or ₹800 drawing board for one course is financially burdening.

Hostel Clutter & Circular Sharing:

Seniors have working gear lying idle. RentEquip creates a peer sharing economy with rentals starting from ₹2/day.

Core Features & 52+ Campus Items

10 Academic Categories:

Stationery, Engineering Drawing, Lab Essentials, Books & Calculators, Electronics, Art & Design, Sports, and Everyday Essentials.

Instant Campus Pickup Locations:

Collect supplies within minutes at Central Library, SAC Desk, Mechanical Workshop, or Hostel Common Rooms.

Transparent Rupee (₹) Pricing:

ENGINEERING DETAILS
Technical Architecture & Implementation
04 / 06
Pure Vanilla Web Implementation
Zero External Dependencies:

100% Native HTML5, modern CSS3 (Flexbox/Grid), and modular vanilla JavaScript. Zero frameworks, npm packages, or build tools.

LocalStorage Persistence Engine:

Client-side repository storing users, 52+ equipment items, bookings, peer reviews, and real-time alerts.

Date Overlap Conflict Engine:

Deterministic interval overlap algorithm (startA ≤ endB && startB ≤ endA) preventing double bookings on confirmed supplies.
